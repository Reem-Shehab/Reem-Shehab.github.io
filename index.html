About

Turning data into clear answers.

I'm a Statistics and Data Science student at the University of Bahrain, finishing General Assembly's full-time Data Science bootcamp. My background is built on statistics first, so I care about doing the analysis properly: understanding the data, choosing the right method, and reading the results honestly.

I work across R, Python, and SQL to clean data, build and compare models, and turn the findings into something a non-technical audience can act on. Most of my projects sit close to real questions in health and public services, which is where I want to keep working.
